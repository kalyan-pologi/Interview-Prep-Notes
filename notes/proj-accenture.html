Project

🏢 Accenture: MyHub Platform Modernization (Intuit Client)

Role: Java Full Stack Engineer · Domain: Internal Business Systems

Large-scale modernization of the client's internal application, used by over 100,000 employees. The original system was outdated, slow, and difficult to maintain, causing performance issues especially during high-traffic periods. Alongside the modernization, the client wanted a new centralized platform called MyHub that would consolidate employee services, leave balances, training, timesheets, payroll, and workspace reservations, into a single portal, since these had previously been scattered across separate applications and were inefficient for employees to navigate.

Migration and Infrastructure

We migrated the system from a monolithic architecture to microservices using Java and Spring Boot, which significantly improved maintainability and performance. I also optimized the PostgreSQL database, improving query speed and data retrieval times. Since the system needed to handle a high volume of users efficiently, we used AWS, EC2 for compute, S3 for storage, and Lambda for background tasks. Docker and Kubernetes containerized and managed the microservices, improving scalability and reducing system downtime by about 15 percent. Apache Kafka and RabbitMQ handled messaging between services for smoother inter-service communication.

Reporting Hierarchy Service

I worked on a Reporting Hierarchy microservice that manages the organizational structure, tracking who reports to whom. It was built with PostgreSQL and GraphQL to store and query hierarchical data efficiently, letting employees quickly look up their reporting chain and letting managers view their direct reports without the slow, denormalized queries the old system relied on.

Overall the project meaningfully improved system performance, maintainability, and the day-to-day employee experience, consolidating scattered internal tools into one place while keeping the platform responsive under high traffic.
